# Contributing

The rules the code is written to are in the README. This file is the part that
is not about code: because CogwareCopilot is dual licensed (see `LICENSING.md`),
every contribution has to be licensed to the copyright holder on terms that
permit relicensing, or the commercial license stops being possible the moment
your patch lands.

## Agreement by contribution

There is nothing to sign. Submitting a contribution *is* your agreement to the
terms below, and they apply from the moment it reaches the project. If you do
not agree to them, do not submit the contribution.
